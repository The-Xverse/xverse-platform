# Design and verification plan

Reworks only the rejected provider slice. CommunicationProvider keeps public read-only identity and descriptor access, while every state-changing provider operation becomes a private virtual entry point callable only by ProviderComposition; independent providers can override private virtuals but external consumers cannot invoke them or mint usable raw authority. Preparation returns the composition-issued public route handle. Activation authenticates that handle, activates the provider resource, then has ProviderComposition activate the exact lifecycle route and returns ProviderStatus. Submission, receive, drain, and close remain composition-mediated. Prior validators retain their complete recursive production, documentation, ownership, lifetime, thread-safety, failure-clause, build, and runtime checks; additive path admission affects ownership only. Final evidence identifies the immutable candidate through SESN host records rather than a worker-known intermediate revision.

- XCOM-PROVIDER-AUTHORITY-REPAIR: Repair R1 in C++ only. Make every state-changing CommunicationProvider and LoopbackProvider operation inaccessible to external consumers and callable only by ProviderComposition, while keeping independent provider overrides possible and public read-only descriptor access intact. Preserve bounded behavior, lock rules, and the implemented composition-owned activation sequence.; checks: VM-XCOM-PROV-LINT
- XCOM-PROVIDER-VERIFICATION-GATE-REPAIR: Repair R2 and R3 after the authority task. Add an external-consumer compile rejection probe, fresh unprepared dispatch-counted mismatch fixtures, the stale/failed/closed/recreated and route-capacity activation matrix, exact diagnostic-byte assertions, and before/after no-mutation checks. Restore every substantive prior recursive source, documentation, ownership, lifetime, thread-safety, failure-clause, build, and runtime gate; additive path admission may affect ownership only.; checks: VM-XCOM-PROV-UNIT, VM-XCOM-PROV-LINT, VM-XCOM-PROV-STATIC, VM-XCOM-PROV-INTEGRATION, VM-XCOM-PROV-VALIDATION
- XCOM-PROVIDER-EVIDENCE-REPAIR: Repair R4 through R6 in the two provider evidence documents. Add reciprocal provider.cpp submission and cleanup allocations, document handle issuance during preparation and composition-owned lifecycle activation, and remove any claim that an intermediate task revision is the final candidate. Bind delivery evidence to the host-identified immutable candidate and preserve maturity.; checks: VM-XCOM-PROV-STATIC
