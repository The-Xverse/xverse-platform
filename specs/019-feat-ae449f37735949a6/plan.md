# Design and verification plan

Add an ObservationHub beside ProviderComposition. The hub owns a fixed tap registry and fixed per-tap record rings. Consumers attach validated filters and payload/overflow policies and receive opaque generation-bound TapHandle values. ProviderComposition optionally references one caller-owned hub. Submit authenticates the provider route and item as before, asks the hub to reserve any lossless-validation capacity before provider dispatch, invokes the provider without an observation lock held, and publishes the resulting accepted or rejected provider outcome afterward. Best-effort taps may drop-newest or coalesce-latest with visible counters and cannot change provider outcome. Lossless-validation capacity failure rejects before provider mutation and marks experiment validity degraded. Consumers poll owned ObservationRecord values and snapshots; no callback, external I/O, storage, or dashboard is introduced.
